// about me

Who I Am

I'm Samiksha Sharma, CEO and Founder of Phoenix IT Solutions, based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I work at the intersection of technology and entrepreneurship, driven by a simple goal—turning ideas into meaningful digital solutions that help businesses grow and adapt in a fast-changing world.

My path began with a curiosity for how software shapes everyday life, which gradually evolved into a mission: to build a company that delivers high-quality IT services while nurturing local talent and expanding Nepal’s presence in the global tech space.

Beyond Phoenix, I invest my time in mentoring aspiring developers, staying engaged with advancements in AI and emerging technologies, and finding inspiration in Nepal’s landscapes—whether through travel, exploration, or discovering new flavors and cuisines.
